# Design System: Joe Cole

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Product-Grade Standard"**

This is the developer portfolio everyone recognises, built the way a good product team builds a marketing page. White ground, near-black ink, a single blue that does every action job, hairline grey borders and 12px corners. Nothing in the system is novel on purpose; the craft is in the precision of what is familiar: optical letter-spacing that tightens as type grows, a three-step ink hierarchy, 1px dividers carrying all the structure, and quiet state transitions on one shared easing curve.

The page opens on one authored stage. A full-viewport portrait stands on its own light studio ground, sampled from the photo's backdrop so the picture and the page are one surface; the name is set across the bottom in giant uppercase, and a dark glass card carries the greeting, the two actions and the availability line. The stage is pinned; everything after it is a white sheet that slides up over it with a soft upward shadow, and the nav floats transparent over the photo until the sheet reaches it.

Density on the sheet is calm and readable. A single 1120px column with a fluid gutter holds every section; sections breathe on a fluid 64 to 112px rhythm and are separated by a hairline, not by colour blocks. Work comes first on the sheet, as screenshots seated in thin browser frames, because the screenshots are the evidence.

The one colour block is the ember ground: a deep rust-to-black glow, built from the hero name's rust and grained like the hero photo, that carries Experience and Contact as the sheet's two dark moments. Experience sets white testimonial-style cards on it; Contact turns its controls white. Experience and Tools open with a centred uppercase display title that echoes the name at section scale.

A dark theme mirrors the light one token for token on the sheet. It follows the operating system by default and can be pinned with a toggle; the accent lightens for text and the photos dim slightly so nothing glares on the dark ground. The hero stage stays light, and the ember ground and its white cards stay as they are, in both themes.

**Key Characteristics:**
- White ground, ink #111827 text, one blue for actions and links.
- A pinned, full-viewport photo stage on a light ground matched to the photo, with the name as the display type; the rest of the page slides over it as a sheet.
- Structure from 1px hairlines; surfaces on the light ground are flat at rest.
- A fixed ember ground (rust glows over near-black, with grain) for Experience and Contact, where actions turn white and cards stay white.
- Two uppercase display steps: the hero name, and the centred display titles that open Experience and Tools.
- Geist for everything readable; Geist Mono only for data (dates, stack tags, URLs in frame bars, tool-card index numerals).
- Corners in a tight family: 6, 8, 9, 10 and 12px, 16px for the hero glass card, plus full circles for dots.
- One easing curve (expo-out) for all motion; 180ms for state changes, one authored hero entrance (photo settles, name rises, card follows), and a scroll grammar built from it: the stage recedes as the sheet covers it, display titles rise word by word like the name, content groups rise once as they arrive.
- Full light and dark parity on the sheet, driven by the same token names; the hero stage is a fixed light island and the ember ground a fixed dark one.

## Colors

A neutral cool-grey ramp with one saturated blue; colour carries meaning, never decoration. The hero stage adds a cool studio grey taken from the photo, and a dark ink glass; the ember ground adds rust glows over near-black, taken from the name.

### Primary
- **Action Blue** (accent; dark: dark-accent): fills the primary button, and nothing else is filled with it. The dark variant is a touch lighter so white button text holds.
- **Pressed Blue** (accent-hover; dark: dark-accent-hover): primary button hover. Darker in light mode, lighter in dark mode, so hover always moves toward contrast with the ground.
- **Link Blue** (accent-text; dark: dark-accent-text): text links, the email address on hover, and the copied state of the copy button. In dark mode it lifts to a soft periwinkle so it reads as text, not as a fill.
- **Blue Wash** (accent-soft; dark: dark-accent-soft): the background of the copy button's confirmed state. Its only use.

### Tertiary
- **Available Green** (ok; dark: dark-ok): the availability status dot and its ping ring. A status signal only; never a button or link. The dot now lives on the hero glass card and follows the ok token (#16a34a light, #22c55e dark).

### Neutral
- **Paper White / Night** (bg; dark: dark-bg): page ground, the sheet, card body, field and secondary button fill, mobile menu panel.
- **Subtle Grey** (bg-subtle): browser-frame chrome, secondary button hover.
- **Muted Grey** (bg-muted): tags, tool cards, icon-button and nav-link hover fill, copy button rest, image placeholders; the experience badge tile and the inverted ember button's hover (fixed light value there).
- **Ink** (ink): headings, primary text, brand mark fill, tool-card brand marks and names.
- **Ink 2** (ink-2): body copy, ledes, summaries, nav links at rest, tag text.
- **Ink 3** (ink-3): metadata, dates, captions, section subheads, footer, frame-bar URLs, tool-card numerals and areas.
- **Hairline** (line): every divider and card border; section separators; nav bottom border once scrolled; the tool card's hover fill.
- **Hairline Strong** (line-strong): secondary button and field borders, card border on hover, frame-bar dots, scrollbar thumb.
- **Selection** (selection): text selection background, a translucent accent.
- **Nav Glass** (nav-bg): the sticky nav's translucent ground under its backdrop blur, once the sheet is under it.
- **Shadow Black** (shadow-black): the pigment of every shadow that has to register on a dark ground: the dark-theme Rest and Lift, the ember card shadow and the ember button's rest shadow, always at an alpha.

### Hero Stage (fixed in both themes)
- **Studio Grey** (hero-ground-top, hero-ground-mid, hero-ground-bottom): the stage ground, a vertical blend top to bottom sampled from the photo backdrop, so the masked edges of the portrait disappear into it.
- **Hero Ink** (hero-ink): the nav's ink while it floats over the stage, and the opaque stop of the photo's edge mask. The light-theme ink value, never inverted.
- **Rust Name** (hero-name): the giant name, and the seed of the ember ground (Ember Glow Deep is the same rust at 80%). A deep rust, warm against the cool grey backdrop, deepened until it holds at least 3:1 on the backdrop (6.6:1), the mustard hoodie (4.2:1) and its shadowed folds (3.1:1); brighter rusts drop below 3:1 over the folds.
- **Ink Glass** (glass-card-bg, with glass-card-border as a 1px white edge): the hero card ground under its blur.
- **On Glass** (on-glass, on-glass-muted): white for the greeting, emphasis and button labels on the card; white at 70% for the intro sentence.
- **On-Dark Outline** (on-dark-line, on-dark-line-hover, on-dark-wash): the border, hover border and hover wash of the outline button on the card.
- **Nav Over Hero** (nav-hero-ink-2, nav-hero-wash, nav-hero-subtle, nav-hero-line, nav-hero-line-strong): the light values the nav pins to while it floats over the stage: link ink, a translucent white hover wash, and soft ink hairlines.

### Ember Ground (fixed in both themes)
- **Ember Glow** (ember-glow): the bright rust glow rising from the bottom right (a 70% by 60% radial at 80% 105%, fading out by 70%).
- **Ember Glow Deep** (ember-glow-deep): the darker rust glow from the top left (55% by 55% at 6% -5%); Rust Name at 80%.
- **Ember Base** (ember-top, ember-mid, ember-bottom): the near-black rust base under both glows, a 165deg linear blend at 0, 55 and 100%.
- **On Ember** (on-ember, on-ember-muted, on-ember-link-hover): white for headings, the email address, text links, the focus ring and the inverted primary fill; white at 72% for the lede; white at 80% for the email address on hover.
- **Ember Controls** (ember-field-bg, ember-field-line, ember-wash, ember-wash-strong): the email field's translucent white fill and border; the copy button at rest, then hovered or copied.
- **Ember Selection** (ember-selection): text selection on the ember ground, with white text.
- **Experience cards** sit on the ember ground in the light theme's fixed values (bg, bg-muted, ink, ink-3, line), never the dark ones.

### Named Rules
**The One Blue Rule.** Blue means "you can act here": the primary button, text links, the focus ring, the caret, the confirmed copy state. There is no decorative blue; on the hero card, emphasis is white weight, not blue, and the only blue on the stage is the Hire me button. The name carries the stage's colour in Rust Name instead. On the ember ground there is no blue at all: the primary button inverts to a white fill with ink text (Muted Grey on hover), text links and the focus ring turn white, and the copied state is a stronger white wash, because blue on rust-black would read as a second accent.

**The Ink Ladder Rule.** Text hierarchy on the sheet is three inks (ink, ink-2, ink-3), never opacity or a fourth grey. The two dark surfaces carry their own two-step ladder: white and white at 70% on the hero glass card, white and white at 72% on the ember ground. The white experience cards on ember use the light ladder's ink and ink-3.

**The Token Parity Rule.** Every colour on the sheet exists in light and dark under the same name; components reference tokens, never raw hex, so the theme flips with no per-component code. The hero stage and the ember ground are the deliberate exceptions: the stage's ground, ink and glass are scoped light values (the nav pins to them while over the stage), and the ember ground and the white cards on it are scoped fixed values, so neither world inverts.

**The Matched Ground Rule.** Every gradient ground is matched to something already on the page. The hero ground is sampled from the photo's own backdrop and the portrait's sides are masked into it; the ember ground is built from the name's rust. A new hero photo means re-sampling the hero ground, and a new name colour means re-deriving the ember glows, never picking a brand colour.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Geist (via next/font, with ui-sans-serif, system-ui fallback)
**Body Font:** Geist
**Label/Mono Font:** Geist Mono (with ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular fallback)

**Character:** One neutral grotesk carries the whole voice; weight and tracking, not a second family, create hierarchy. Mono appears only where the content is data.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (650, 20.2vw, 0.8, uppercase): the name across the bottom of the hero, the page's h1. Each word sits in its own clip so it can rise; never wraps; Rust Name in both themes.
- **Hello** (620, 28px, 1.1; 24px at 720px and below): the greeting on the hero card, in white. Shares the headline's weight and tracking.
- **Display Title** (700, fluid 44 to 88px, 0.92, uppercase, centred): the titles that open Experience ("Experience & education") and Tools ("Tools & expertise"), an echo of the name at section scale. Balanced wrapping, with the "& …" phrase held together; 40 to 72px (fluid) above the content. Inherits ink on the sheet and white on ember.
- **Headline** (620, fluid 28 to 40px, 1.1): section titles for Work, About and Contact. Balanced wrapping.
- **Title** (600, 19px, 1.3): project names. Experience card titles use 600 at body size (-0.01em).
- **Quote** (500, fluid 17 to 20px, 1.45): the description on the large experience cards, in ink, pretty wrapping; Quote Small fixes it at 17px on the education cards.
- **Lede** (400, fluid 17 to 20px, 1.55): the contact intro line, ink-2, max 52ch.
- **Body** (400, 17px, 1.6): page default; prose paragraphs in ink-2, max 62ch, pretty wrapping.
- **Body Small** (400, 15px, 1.55): project summaries, the organisation line on experience cards (ink-3), and the hero card intro (On Glass muted, with role and location in white at 550).
- **Label** (550, 15px, -0.005em): buttons and text links; nav links at 15px regular; copy button at 14px 550. The "Also" label before the skills tags is body size at 600.
- **Label Caps** (600, 13px, +0.02em, uppercase): tool names on tool cards.
- **Meta** (400, 14px): project kind, notes, footer, the hero card status line; 13px for photo captions and tool areas.
- **Mono** (400, 13px, tabular numerals): dates on experience cards. Tags and tool-card numerals at 12px; frame-bar URL at 11px.
- **Badge initials** (700, 19px, -0.02em; 15px on the small cards): the initials in an experience badge tile.

### Named Rules
**The Tightening Rule.** Tracking tightens as size grows: -0.04em display and display title, -0.03em headline and hello, -0.015em quote, -0.012em title, -0.01em for emphasized body lines, 0 for mono; only small uppercase labels open up, to +0.02em.

**The One Name Rule.** The name is the only type at display size: Geist 650, uppercase, 20.2vw, edge to edge, once, on the hero stage. The display title is its echo, not its rival: Geist 700, uppercase, centred, capped at 88px, and only to open a showcase section (Experience, Tools). Every other section title stays at headline in sentence case, and nothing else is uppercase above label size.

**The Mono-Is-Data Rule.** Geist Mono is reserved for dates, stack tags, the URL in a browser frame and the index numerals on tool cards. Never for headings, labels or prose.

## Layout

One centred container (max 1120px plus gutters on each side; gutter fluid 16 to 32px). Sections use a fluid vertical padding of 64 to 112px; each section after the first carries a 1px hairline top border. Section heads sit on a flex row (title and subhead left, an optional text link right, aligned to the baseline end), with a fluid 28 to 44px gap before content.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat at rest on the sheet, hairlines for structure, with two soft shadow steps. Controls that sit on the page (secondary button, primary button, email field) carry a barely-there 1px shadow; the deep shadow appears only when a project card is hovered. In dark mode the same two names deepen to near-black so they still register. The hero is the layered exception: grain over photo over ground, a floating glass card, and the sheet's edge shadow as it covers the stage. The ember ground is the second: grain over gradient, with white cards floating on it.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Rest** (shadow-sm, light `0 1px 2px rgb(17 24 39 / 0.05)`, dark `0 1px 2px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.4)`): buttons and the email field, to seat them on the ground.
- **Lift** (shadow-md, light `0 12px 28px -12px rgb(17 24 39 / 0.18), 0 2px 6px -2px rgb(17 24 39 / 0.06)`, dark `0 16px 32px -14px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.7), 0 2px 6px -2px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.4)`): project card hover only.
- **Primary sheen** (`inset 0 1px 0 rgb(255 255 255 / 0.14)`, stacked with Rest): the top-edge highlight on the primary button.
- **Glass float** (`0 28px 56px -28px rgb(17 24 39 / 0.55)`): the hero card, at rest, over the photo.
- **Sheet edge** (`0 -24px 48px -24px rgb(17 24 39 / 0.18)`): the top edge of the sheet as it slides over the stage.
- **Ember card** (`0 30px 60px -32px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.6)`): the white experience cards, at rest, on the ember ground.
- **Ember button rest** (`0 1px 2px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.2)`): the inverted primary button on ember, in place of Rest.
- **Film grain** (a fractal-noise SVG tile): over the hero photo and ground at 0.22 opacity in overlay blend, and over the ember ground at 0.07 in plain alpha (no blend mode, because the glow underneath drifts and a blend would be recomputed every frame). Nowhere else.

### Named Rules
**The Flat-At-Rest Rule.** Cards on the light ground sit flat; lift is a hover response, never a resting state. Project cards lift with shadow-md plus a 2px rise; tool cards lift by tone, their fill darkening to Hairline as they rise 2px, with no shadow. Two things float at rest because they sit on a picture, not on a hairline: the hero glass card over the photo, and the white experience cards over the ember glow.

**The Two Glass Rule.** Glass is reserved for the two things that float: the sticky nav (nav-bg under `saturate(180%) blur(14px)`; transparent with no blur while over the stage, opaque when the mobile menu opens) and the hero card (Ink Glass under `blur(20px) saturate(140%)`). There is no third glass surface; the translucent email field on ember is a tint with no blur, not glass.

## Shapes

A tight, soft-cornered family. 16px for the hero glass card, the one larger corner; 12px for containers (project cards, experience and tool cards, about photo, email field, contact button), 10px for standard buttons and experience badge tiles, 9px for icon buttons, 8px for small controls (nav links, nav CV button, copy button, brand mark, skip link), 6px for tags and the focus ring. Full circles for the status dot, the frame-bar dots and the project-note dot; 999px pills only for the scrollbar thumb. The hero portrait is unframed: no radius, no ring, its sides feathered into the ground by a horizontal mask. Each word of the hero name is clipped by its own box so it can rise into place. Images are clipped by their container's radius; screenshots crop from the top. Brand marks (Simple Icons, CC0) are single-colour SVG drawn in the current text colour, never in their brand colours.

### Motion
- **Easing:** one curve, expo-out `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`, for everything.
- **Durations:** 160ms skip link; 180ms buttons, links, icon buttons, nav links, copy button; 220ms project card and tool card; 320ms tool mark scale; 240ms nav border; 360ms mobile panel; 380ms copy confirm; 600ms screenshot zoom; 900ms hero card; 1100ms hero name; 1600ms hero photo.
- **Hero entrance:** one authored moment that plays once on load. The photo settles from 1.06 scale, 10px blur and zero opacity to rest (1600ms); each word of the name rises from below its clip (1100ms, 110ms stagger after a 250ms delay); the card rises 10px from 6px blur and zero opacity (900ms, 650ms delay).
- **Status ping:** the availability dot emits a fading ring every 2.4s after a 1.8s delay.
- **Stage recede (scroll-linked):** while the sheet slides over the pinned hero, a 0–1 progress (`--hero-p`, set once per frame by Motion.tsx) scales the photo up to 1.08, lifts the name by up to 12vh and dims the whole stage under a #0d0403 veil up to 0.55.
- **Title rise:** display titles split into words, each in its own clip; the first time a title scrolls in, its words rise from 110% below (1000ms, 120ms stagger), the same gesture as the hero name.
- **Group reveal:** content groups (section heads, project grid, About text, experience grids, tool grid, Also row, Contact) rise 28px from zero opacity the first time they scroll in (900ms), children one after another (70ms stagger, capped at 7). Groups already on screen when the page loads never hide.
- **Photo wipe:** the About photo is wiped up from the bottom of its frame (clip-path, 1100ms) while settling from 1.15 scale (1500ms). The clip sits on the image, never on the observed frame.
- **Ember drift:** the ember glow layer drifts by -5%/-4% and scales to 1.1 over 16s, alternating, and runs only while its section is on screen (`data-live`), with `will-change` only then.
- **Nav highlight:** a muted pill slides under the link of the section at the reading line (about 40% down the screen), 450ms on translate and width; the link reads in ink and carries `aria-current="location"`.
- **Theme switch:** a view transition; the new theme is revealed by a circle growing from the toggle (650ms). The toggle icon turns in (rotate -90°, 0.5 scale, 500ms).
- **Mobile menu:** links cascade in, 12px rise from zero opacity, 520ms, 45ms apart after 90ms.
- **Tool marks:** on hover a mark takes its brand colour (mixed 60% with white in dark mode), 260ms.
- **Reduced motion:** every entrance, reveal, recede, drift, cascade and the view transition sit behind `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference` (or are skipped in script); groups show at once, smooth scrolling turns off, the nav highlight fades instead of sliding, and colour and state transitions remain. Content is visible by default and only waits for its entrance once the motion script is running.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** reference colour tokens by name so light and dark stay in parity; add any new colour to both theme blocks.
- **Do** keep the hero stage light in both themes, its ground re-sampled from the photo backdrop and the name in Rust Name.
- **Do** keep Action Blue as a fill for the primary button only, and Link Blue for text links.
- **Do** separate light sections and rows with 1px hairlines (line) and keep cards on the light ground flat until hover; ember sections drop the hairline on both edges.
- **Do** keep the ember ground identical in both themes, with white text, a white focus ring and the inverted white primary button, and keep experience cards white on it.
- **Do** draw brand marks as single-colour SVG in currentColor.
- **Do** use Geist Mono only for dates, stack tags, frame-bar URLs and tool-card numerals.
- **Do** show product screenshots inside the browser frame, cropped from the top at 16:10.
- **Do** run every transition on the expo-out curve; 180ms for state changes.
- **Do** keep text in the three-step ink ladder, with measure capped at 52 to 64ch.

### Don't:
- **Don't** introduce a second accent hue, decorative gradients or gradient text. There are exactly two gradient grounds, each matched to the page: the hero's photo-sampled ground and the ember ground built from the name's rust. Green is a status signal only.
- **Don't** put blue on the ember ground; actions there are white.
- **Don't** add a third glass surface; glass belongs to the nav and the hero card.
- **Don't** give any heading the hero name's display size; uppercase headings are the name and the display titles that open Experience and Tools, nothing else.
- **Don't** show skills as meters, bars or percentages; skills are tool cards and tags.
- **Don't** rest a shadow on a card on the light ground; the Ember card shadow belongs to the white cards on ember.
- **Don't** add looping animation beyond the availability ping and the ember drift, run either loop off screen, or replay any entrance on scroll; every reveal plays once.
- **Don't** rest shadow-md on anything; it is the project card's hover state.
- **Don't** set display type in Geist Mono or a second sans.
